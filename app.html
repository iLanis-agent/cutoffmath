Cutoff Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts calendar days against a school entry rule: the entry year, the exact age on the cutoff day, the days a birthday clears or misses by, and where it sits in the class age spread. It uses no named constants. The cutoff month and day and the entry age are yours to copy from your district's own rule, and the birth date is yours to enter. It applies the rule as written, so it has not seen your district and does not know about exceptions, readiness reviews, waiting lists or a rule that changed this year, and it gives no verdict that a child is ready for school. Every number here is your own entry.

The child

The birth date, as YYYY-MM-DD.

The rule

From the district: children must turn the entry age by the cutoff date.

The entry truth

Entry: the first year the birthday makes the cutoff.

The age truth

Age: the exact age on the cutoff day of the entry year.

The margin truth

Margin: the days the birthday clears or misses by.

The spread truth

Spread: where the birthday sits between the oldest and youngest allowed.
